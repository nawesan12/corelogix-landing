# How Implementation Teams Scale with Grow ERP

Taking a customer live used to mean juggling shared spreadsheets, status emails, and late-night syncs across departments. With Grow ERP, implementation leads finally have a centralized workspace that keeps every project moving.

## Kickoff that sets the tone

- Share onboarding tasks with the customer in a branded portal.
- Assign internal owners to critical steps and visualize dependencies on a shared timeline.
- Surface blockers early with health indicators that update automatically.

## Standardize what works

Our template library includes launch plans for manufacturing, wholesale, and services teams. Duplicate a template, tweak the milestones, and every new customer starts with a proven path to value.

Executives get a live portfolio view across every implementation. Dashboards track health scores, projected go-live dates, and capacity needs, eliminating end-of-week slide decks.
