# Introducing Operational Intelligence in Grow ERP

Grow ERP was designed to be more than a place to store transactions. Operational intelligence is about connecting the dots between data, people, and decisions so teams can move faster with confidence.

## A shared workspace for every team

- Sales reps receive a summarized view of open deals, flagged risks, and follow-up recommendations.
- Finance teams can watch cash flow trends and get alerted before variances become problems.
- Operations leaders track fulfillment velocity, inventory health, and supplier performance in a single screen.

By surfacing the right information at the right moment, Grow ERP removes manual reporting loops and replaces them with live dashboards everyone can trust.

## Automations that remove repetitive work

We have added visual automations that respond to business events as they happen. Create rules that:

1. Notify account managers when high-value opportunities stall.
2. Escalate purchase orders if suppliers miss promised dates.
3. Synchronize inventory thresholds across every location.

No more copying data between tools or chasing status updates — Grow ERP now keeps your processes aligned automatically.

>
> — Priya Nair, COO at BrightGoods

## What’s next

Operational intelligence is a journey. In the coming weeks you will see deeper predictive metrics, AI-assisted forecasting, and a growing library of automation templates built from customer feedback.

If you are already using Grow ERP, head to **Workspace → Intelligence** to explore the new capabilities. New to Grow ERP? [Book a demo](https://example.com/demo) and we will walk you through the playbook.
